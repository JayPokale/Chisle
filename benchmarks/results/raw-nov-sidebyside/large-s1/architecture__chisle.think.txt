**Ordering:**

- Don't pick the most tightly coupled (hard to extract)
- Start with low-coupling, low-risk services to build muscle memory
- Then move to higher-risk/higher-value services once you have the pattern

**Data ownership:**
- Dual-write pattern initially
- Synchronization challenges
- Event sourcing / changelog capture
- Gradual traffic shift
- Eventually remove duplication

**Keeping shippable:**

- Rollback always available
