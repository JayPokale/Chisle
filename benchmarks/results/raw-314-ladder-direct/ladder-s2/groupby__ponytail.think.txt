Let me show the simplest version that works:

```python
from collections import defaultdict

orders = [
    {"customer_id": 1, "amount": 100},
    {"customer_id": 2, "amount": 50},
    {"customer_id": 1, "amount": 75},
    {"customer_id": 3, "amount": 200},
    {"customer_id": 2, "amount": 25},
]

customer_totals = defaultdict(float)
for order in orders:
    customer_totals[order["customer_id"]] += order["amount"]

print(dict(customer_totals))
# Output: {1: 175.0, 2: 75.0, 3: 200.0}
```
