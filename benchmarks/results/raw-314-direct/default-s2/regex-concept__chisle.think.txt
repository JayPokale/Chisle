A backreference lets you refer back to a captured group within the same regex pattern or in a replacement string.

Example: match repeated words
Pattern: `(\w+)\s+\1` matches a word followed by the same word again (like "hello hello")
- `(\w+)` captures a word into group 1
- `\1` refers back to whatever group 1 captured
