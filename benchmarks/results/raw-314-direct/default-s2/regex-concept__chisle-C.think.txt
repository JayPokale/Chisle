A backreference refers to a previously captured group in the same regex pattern. It's written as `\1`, `\2`, etc. (or `\g<name>` for named groups).

Example: matching repeated words like "the the" or "hello hello".

Pattern: `(\w+) \1` matches a word followed by space and the same word again.
