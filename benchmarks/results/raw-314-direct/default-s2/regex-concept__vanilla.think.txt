A backreference in a regex is a way to refer back to a captured group within the same pattern. It lets you match the same text that was previously matched by a group.

The syntax is typically `\1`, `\2`, etc. in the regex pattern itself, referring to groups 1, 2, etc.

A common example: matching repeated words or finding duplicate patterns.
