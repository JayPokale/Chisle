**REST:**
- Pros: Simple, well-understood, caching is straightforward (HTTP caching), good for simple CRUD operations
- Cons: Over-fetching (getting more data than needed), under-fetching (need multiple requests), versioning complexity

**GraphQL:**
- Pros: Clients request exactly what they need, single endpoint, strong typing, good for complex data requirements
- Cons: More complex to implement, caching is harder, query complexity issues, steeper learning curve
