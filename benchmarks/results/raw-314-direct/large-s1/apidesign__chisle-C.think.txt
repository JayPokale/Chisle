## Resources

```
/tenants/{tenantId}/documents
/tenants/{tenantId}/documents/{docId}
/tenants/{tenantId}/documents/{docId}/versions
/tenants/{tenantId}/documents/{docId}/versions/{versionId}
/tenants/{tenantId}/members
/tenants/{tenantId}/members/{userId}
```

**Key decisions:**
- Tenant in path enforces isolation at the routing layer
- Flat document structure (no nested folders) — simplifies API, versioning, and permissions
- Versions as a sub-resource of documents
- Members tied to tenant (not global users)
