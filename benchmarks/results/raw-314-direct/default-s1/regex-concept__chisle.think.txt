A backreference in regex refers back to a captured group earlier in the pattern. It's written as `\1`, `\2`, etc. (or sometimes `$1`, `$2` depending on context).

Simple example: match doubled words. Pattern: `(\w+)\s+\1` matches "hello hello" or "word word" but not "hello goodbye".
