A backreference refers to a previously captured group in a regex pattern. In the replacement or within the pattern itself, you can refer back to what was captured.

Format: `\1`, `\2`, etc. in most regex flavors (or `$1`, `$2` in replacements in some contexts).

Short example: `(\w+)\s+\1` matches repeated words like "hello hello".
