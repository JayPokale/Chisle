A backreference refers to a previously captured group in a regex pattern, typically written as `\1`, `\2`, etc., or in some languages `$1`, `$2`.

Simple example: matching duplicate words like "hello hello" - the pattern would be `(\w+)\s+\1` where `\1` refers back to the first capture group.
