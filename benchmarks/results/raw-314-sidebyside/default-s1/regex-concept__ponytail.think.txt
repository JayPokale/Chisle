A backreference in regex refers to a previously captured group in the same regex pattern. You denote them with `\1`, `\2`, etc. (or `$1`, `$2` in some contexts like replacements).

Common use case: finding repeated words, like `\b(\w+)\s+\1\b` matches a word followed by the same word.
