A backreference in regex refers back to a previously captured group in the same pattern. It's denoted as `\1`, `\2`, etc. (or `\g<1>`, `\g<name>` in some engines).

One short example: matching repeated words, like "hello hello" or "world world".

Pattern: `(\w+) \1` matches a word followed by itself (with space).
