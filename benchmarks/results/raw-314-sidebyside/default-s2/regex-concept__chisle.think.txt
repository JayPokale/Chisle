Backreference = a way to refer back to a captured group within the same regex pattern. Used to match repeated text or find duplicates.

Example: matching doubled words like "hello hello" or "the the"
Pattern: `(\w+)\s+\1` captures a word, matches spaces, then refers back to that same captured group.
