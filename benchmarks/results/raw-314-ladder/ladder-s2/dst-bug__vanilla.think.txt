The user is describing a problem where a nightly job drifts by an hour after every daylight saving time (DST) change. The job is scheduled using `setInterval(run, 24 * 60 * 60 * 1000)`, which schedules it to run every 86,400,000 milliseconds.

This is a classic DST bug. The issue is that `setInterval` doesn't account for daylight saving time changes. When DST changes occur:

1. When clocks "spring forward" (lose an hour), the actual elapsed time for a 24-hour period in wall-clock time is actually 25 hours (86,400,000 ms represents 24 hours of actual time, but on a DST change day, 24 hours of actual time spans more than 24 hours of wall-clock time)

2. When clocks "fall back" (gain an hour), the opposite happens - 24 hours of actual time spans less than 24 hours of wall-clock time
