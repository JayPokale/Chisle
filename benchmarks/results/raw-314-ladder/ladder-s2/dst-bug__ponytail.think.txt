This is a classic issue with daylight saving time (DST) transitions. Let me understand the problem:

1. A nightly job is scheduled using `setInterval(run, 24 * 60 * 60 * 1000)` - this repeats every 24 hours (in milliseconds)
2. After every daylight saving time change, the job drifts by an hour

The root cause: `setInterval` doesn't account for daylight saving time. When DST transitions happen:
- In spring (clocks go forward): The day is effectively 23 hours, but `setInterval` still waits 24 hours of wall-clock time, so the job runs an hour later.
- In fall (clocks go back): The day is effectively 25 hours, but `setInterval` still waits 24 hours of wall-clock time, so the job runs an hour earlier.

Actually, let me think about this more carefully. `setInterval(run, 24 * 60 * 60 * 1000)` schedules the function to run every 86,400,000 milliseconds. This is an absolute time interval - it doesn't depend on what the clock says.

When DST happens:

- Fall back (2:00 AM → 1:00 AM): The wall clock goes back an hour, but the interval is still 86,400,000 ms. So the job might run an hour earlier.
