The user is describing a classic daylight saving time (DST) bug. They have a nightly job scheduled with `setInterval(run, 24 * 60 * 60 * 1000)` and it drifts by an hour after every DST change.

The root cause here is that `setInterval` with a fixed millisecond interval doesn't account for daylight saving time changes. When a DST transition occurs:
- Spring forward: 1 hour is "skipped" - clocks jump ahead, so the interval actually covers 25 hours of wall-clock time
- Fall back: 1 hour is repeated - clocks fall back, so the interval covers 23 hours of wall-clock time

This causes the job to drift relative to the wall-clock time it's supposed to run.
